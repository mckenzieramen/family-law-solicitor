Book a free 30-minute consultation

Your free consultation can be held by phone, by video or in person. It is a chance to:

  • explain what has happened and what you are hoping to achieve
  • get an initial view on your options and the likely next steps
  • understand any time limits that may apply to you
  • find out how Prevail Family Law can help, and what it is likely to cost.

Getting the most from your consultation

You don’t need to prepare anything, but it helps to have the following on hand if you can:

  • the date you married or started living together, and the date you separated
  • the names and dates of birth of any children
  • a rough list of assets, debts and superannuation, for property matters
  • any court documents, orders, parenting plans or agreements you already have
  • any letters from your former partner or their lawyer.

If you need help urgently

If you or your children are in immediate danger, call the police on 000.

For confidential support about domestic, family or sexual violence, you can contact 1800RESPECT on 1800 737 732, 24 hours a day.

If you have a court date coming up soon, or are worried a child may be taken overseas, say so when you get in touch so your enquiry can be prioritised.

Your privacy

Everything you tell Prevail Family Law is treated confidentially. Contacting Prevail Family Law, or attending a free consultation, does not by itself create a lawyer–client relationship. Please don’t send confidential documents until you have spoken with us.
