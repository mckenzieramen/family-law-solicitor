About Us

About Prevail Family Law

At Prevail Family Law, we provide practical, compassionate legal guidance to individuals and families navigating separation, divorce, parenting arrangements and property settlements. We understand that family law matters are personal, and that clear advice can make a meaningful difference at a challenging time.

Our approach begins with listening. We take the time to understand each client’s circumstances, explain their legal options and develop a considered strategy that reflects their priorities. Whether a matter can be resolved through negotiation or mediation, or requires court proceedings, we focus on helping clients make informed decisions and move forward with confidence.

Supporting clients across Sydney, including the Eastern Suburbs, North Sydney and the Northern Beaches, Prevail Family Law is committed to approachable communication, discretion and thoughtful legal support.

Our why

Separation is one of the most difficult experiences a person can go through. Too often, people feel lost in the process: passed between lawyers, unsure of the costs and unclear about what happens next.

Prevail Family Law was created to change that. The aim is simple: to give every client clear advice, genuine support and a practical path forward, so they can protect what matters most and get on with the next chapter of their lives.

Your solicitor

Meet Melissa Gok

Family Law Solicitor

Melissa Gok is a Sydney-based family law solicitor with over 10 years’ experience in family law. She holds a Bachelor of Laws and a Bachelor of Commerce (majoring in Finance) from Macquarie University and worked with established Sydney law firms before founding Prevail Family Law.

Her experience includes parenting arrangements and disputes, property settlements, financial agreements and dispute resolution. She understands the emotional and financial pressures that can accompany separation and approaches each matter with care, attention to detail and practical judgement.

Melissa’s aim is to help you reach a fair and appropriate outcome as quickly and as inexpensively as possible. She helps clients understand their options and make informed decisions, combining clear communication and thoughtful negotiation with a readiness to navigate court processes when required.
